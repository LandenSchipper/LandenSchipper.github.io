Understanding Shading and Value

Value refers to the lightness or darkness of a color or shade. In art, manipulating value creates the illusion of depth, form, and light. Proper shading involves a smooth transition from light to dark areas, helping transform a flat image into a three-dimensional one. This piece showcases a wide range of values to define its structure and mood.
